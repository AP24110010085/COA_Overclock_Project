Hardware Mathematical Foundations

1. Clock Frequency ($f$):

f = Base Clock (BCLK) × Multiplier

2. Dynamic CMOS Power ($P$):

P = Dynamic Capacitance (C) × Voltage (V²) × Frequency (f)

3. Thermal Dissipation Model ($T_j$):

T_junction = T_ambient + (Power × Thermal Resistance R_th)

4. Voltage Stability Bound ($V_{\text{min}}$):

V_min = V_stock + α × (f_target - f_stock) [Setup Time Protection]
